# /trader-analyst — Indian Stock Market Earnings & Financial Analyst

Analyzes quarterly results and fundamental parameters for Indian equities:
- **Quarterly Report Analysis**: Net Sales, EBITDA margin, PAT (Profit After Tax in ₹ Crores), and EPS across quarters (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4).
- **YoY and QoQ Growth**: Evaluates acceleration or margin deceleration.
- **Valuation Benchmarks**: P/E ratio relative to sector peers, Return on Equity (ROE %), and dividend yield.

## Usage

```bash
# Analyze quarterly earnings and valuation for RELIANCE
python3 trader-analyst/facts.py RELIANCE

# Analyze TCS financial facts
python3 trader-analyst/facts.py TCS
```
